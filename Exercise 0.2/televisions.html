Energy-saving tips

Reducing TV running costs

Tip 01

Turn off at the wall

Standby power is usually small for a single device, but switching appliances off when they are not needed can help reduce unnecessary electricity use.

Tip 02

Lower the brightness

Reducing screen brightness can help lower the power consumed by some television displays.

Tip 03

Match size to the room

Larger panels generally draw more power regardless of technology.
