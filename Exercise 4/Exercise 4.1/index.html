Why it matters

Small appliances, big bills

Across Australian homes, entertainment and standby devices are often left running for hours longer than people realise. This page collects general information on appliance energy use, with televisions used as the running example for this exercise. All figures on this site are illustrative placeholder values, not verified market data.
